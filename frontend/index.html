      Privacy

      What is stored and where your messages go.

      • Chats and uploaded documents are stored on the server under your user ID. Delete a chat from the sidebar, and a document from My Documents in the message box.
      • To answer, your message and relevant excerpts of your documents are sent to the provider of the selected model (Groq or Google).
      • When web search is used, the search text is sent to Tavily.
      • Theme, text size, spacing and notification preferences are saved in this browser only.
